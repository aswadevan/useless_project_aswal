### Project Description
ARIYATHE THALAYATTIYATH is a humorous AI computer vision web application that uses real-time webcam facial tracking (MediaPipe) and machine learning to analyze head-nodding behavior. It classifies your nodding into Genuine, Polite, Danger, or Survival modes to reveal whether you actually understand what is being explained or are just nodding along to survive the conversation. The app calculates a real-time "Understanding Score" and issues a hilarious diagnostic certificate of cranial agreement

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
In college lectures, technical vivas, and meetings, people suffer from the involuntary reflex of vigorously nodding their head and saying *"athe... athe..."* even when their brain has registered a complete 404 error. Until now, there was no scientific tool to catch people faking cognitive comprehension.


### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We built an AI-powered Cranial Comprehension HUD that extracts 9 kinematic head-movement features (pitch velocity, cadence, and jerk acceleration) from 468 3D facial landmarks to mathematically expose fake understanding in real time.
